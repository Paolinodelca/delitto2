# IMAGO Interview — Private Beta User Experience

## UX objective
Let the user begin quickly, understand the immediate purpose and make no more than two or three decisions per step.

## Entry

### Step 1 — Identity state
- Create a new Professional Identity
- Recover an existing Professional Identity

### Step 2 — Working mode
- Work independently
- Work with a tutor

If tutor collaboration is selected, the person grants explicit access.

### Step 3 — Immediate goal

Candidate examples:
- understand how I appear professionally;
- prepare for an interview;
- improve or create a CV.

Tutor examples:
- organize candidate information;
- build a clear professional narrative;
- prepare the candidate.

## Candidate path
The candidate may:
- upload heterogeneous source material;
- choose interview with or without a target role;
- choose a simple interview length;
- optionally choose interviewer style;
- receive a printable report;
- request a target-aligned or general CV proposal;
- request a cover letter;
- save a dated Professional Identity snapshot.

## Tutor path
The tutor enters through an independent workspace and sees only authorized candidates.

The tutor may:
- review and integrate source material;
- organize professional history;
- identify strengths, gaps and evidence needs;
- support target definition;
- prepare interviews and positioning;
- propose useful development actions;
- explore plausible professional directions.

## Help pattern
Primary screens stay minimal. Additional explanations appear only when requested.

## Beta limits
The Private Beta may limit supported role families, interview styles, outputs, collaboration permissions and persistence features.
