# IMAGO Interview — Private Beta Experience

## Experience objective
The Beta must feel like a real, guided product with low cognitive load.

The user should never wonder what to do next.

## Primary candidate flow
```text
Access
→ Immediate goal
→ Privacy and consent
→ Authorised material acquisition
→ Session preparation
→ Interview
→ Contextual feedback
→ Professional Perception report
→ Final feedback
→ Dated Professional Identity snapshot
```

Consent always precedes CV, documents or other personal material.

## Entry and identity
The product identifies whether the person is creating or recovering a Professional Identity.

Primary choices remain limited to two or three per step.

## Working mode
The person may work:
- independently;
- with a Tutor.

Selecting Tutor mode grants no access. Tutor authorization is a separate explicit and revocable action.

## Immediate goals
The Beta may expose a small subset such as:
- understand how I currently emerge professionally;
- prepare for a target interview or role;
- update my professional picture.

Unimplemented capabilities must not be promised.

## Material acquisition
The product accepts available authorised material without requiring unnecessary completeness.

The candidate may start from free/conversational professional input and simple document upload. CV is a common source, not the conceptual boundary of the product; target/job material and other useful authorised professional sources remain optional.

The user is not required to classify material according to IMAGO's internal source, Evidence or Knowledge taxonomy. Internal acquisition remains structured and provenance-preserving.

A lightweight contextual feedback question may follow the material-preparation step.

## Interview opening
The interview always begins with a free professional presentation.

This first answer establishes initial narrative and credibility evidence and influences what needs further exploration.

## Interview behaviour
The interview is adaptive, not a fixed questionnaire.

Question selection prioritizes:
1. role and target relevance;
2. evidence gaps and ambiguities;
3. seniority calibration;
4. contextual perception objectives;
5. interviewer-style delivery.

Follow-up questions are used only when target observability is insufficient.

Voice is preferred when available. Text remains supported.

Response timing may be recorded as an internal signal. It is never shown as an autonomous judgement.

Interrupt/resume may exist for operational necessity, but the experience should discourage unnecessary interruption.

Explanatory detours during the interview are avoided; deeper explanation belongs primarily in the report.

## Interviewer style
A small Beta set may expose styles such as standard, supportive or incisive.

Style changes tone, pressure and follow-up sharpness, not the underlying evidence model.

## Interview feedback
At interview completion, the candidate reviews the actual questions asked.

Preferred prompts explore:
- which question was surprising or unexpected;
- which question created useful reflection;
- what the candidate expected but was not asked.

This is more informative than asking the candidate to validate technical relevance.

## Report
The first report view communicates only a few strong messages:
- how the candidate currently emerges;
- strongest credibility assets;
- main distance or risk for the target;
- a maximum of three useful next actions.

Detailed evidence, answer analysis and explanation are progressively disclosed.

The report should help the user see what they were not seeing, not force them to read every available analysis.

## Report feedback
Immediately after the first report experience, collect a small number of contextual signals such as:
- degree of self-recognition;
- usefulness;
- what feels missing or wrong.

## Final feedback
Only at the end ask general questions such as reuse, recommendation and optional free comment.

Feedback never modifies the Professional Identity.

## Tutor path
Tutor access is independent and permission-based.

A Tutor may eventually:
- view authorized candidate material and Representation;
- contribute attributable material or notes;
- support target definition;
- review report outputs;
- initiate preparation activities within granted permissions.

The person remains the owner.

## Beta rollout
Prefer one platform with two controlled cohorts:
- Candidate Beta: primary end-to-end path;
- Tutor-assisted Beta: smaller controlled cohort to validate collaboration and outplacement value.

The Tutor path may be enabled incrementally without changing the ownership model.


## Professional Identity continuity for the Beta

The First Human Test must treat the experience as more than a one-session `CV + target → interview → report → end` flow. The minimum Product cycle is `ACQUIRE → UNDERSTAND → HELP → PRESERVE → REUSE`.

For the Beta:
- the person creates or recovers the existing Professional Identity;
- authorised acquisition may begin from a current CV, free-form professional input and, when available, additional professional material;
- target/job material is a separate context and is not part of person identity;
- the user is shown what IMAGO currently understands with provenance-aware explanation and can identify material errors where the implemented experience supports correction;
- immediate utility prioritizes professional/CV representation feedback and interview preparation/feedback;
- supported new Evidence from interaction may contribute to reusable person knowledge through authorised semantic paths;
- the Professional Identity must be preservable and reopenable for later enrichment;
- already-authorised information should be reusable with another target without requiring reconstruction from zero.

The Beta Product requirement is `SAVE → REOPEN → ENRICH → REUSE`. It does not prescribe persistence technology.

### Candidate and professional-support perspectives

Use one platform and one canonical person-owned Professional Identity with controlled perspectives:
- **Candidate / Professional:** understand and improve professional representation, prepare materials/interviews and reuse accumulated supported knowledge.
- **Tutor / Outplacement / Career Professional:** with explicit revocable authorization, organize heterogeneous material, inspect what is supported or insufficiently observed, identify areas to investigate, and support CV/interview/target work.

These perspectives may have different views and actions but do not create separate Knowledge engines. The human professional retains judgment and responsibility.

### Report and living application

A report/export is a dated **snapshot**. The application and Professional Identity are the **living reusable professional representation/workspace**. Export must not be deliberately restricted to force return use. Return value should come from preservation, enrichment and reuse of supported professional knowledge.

### First Human Test product scope

AR-03 opened the technical gate; the First Human Test is now a Product test. It should evaluate minimum utility, acquisition/usability, correctness of understood professional material, useful CV/interview support, continuity/reuse potential, the candidate perspective and, where practical, the Tutor/outplacement perspective. The detailed protocol is defined separately from this Product Authority.
