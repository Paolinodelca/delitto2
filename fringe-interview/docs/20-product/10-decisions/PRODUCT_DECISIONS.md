# Product Decisions

---

## PD-001 — Consent before personal data

**Status:** CANONICAL
**Decision:** No personal data is acquired before explicit user consent.

---

## PD-002 — Professional Identity ownership

**Status:** CANONICAL
**Decision:** The Professional Identity always belongs to the person. Tutor access is always explicit and revocable.

---

## PD-003 — Interview opening

**Status:** CANONICAL
**Decision:** Every interview begins with a free presentation of the candidate's professional journey.

---

## PD-004 — Adaptive probing

**Status:** CANONICAL
**Decision:** Follow-up questions are generated only when a target dimension has not reached sufficient observability.

---

## PD-005 — Minimum intrusiveness

**Status:** CANONICAL
**Decision:** Once sufficient observability has been reached, a dimension is no longer explored.

---

## PD-006 — Continuous primary flow

**Status:** CANONICAL
**Decision:** The primary journey remains continuous. Explanations appear only on explicit request.

---

## PD-007 — Contextual feedback

**Status:** CANONICAL
**Decision:** Feedback is collected immediately after the experience to which it refers. General feedback is requested only at the end.

---

## PD-008 — Progressive reporting

**Status:** CANONICAL
**Decision:** Reports first communicate the highest-value messages. Details are progressively disclosed.

---

## PD-009 — Preferred interaction mode

**Status:** CANONICAL
**Decision:** Voice is the preferred interaction mode whenever available. Text remains fully supported.

---

## PD-010 — Behavioural signals

**Status:** CANONICAL
**Decision:** Response timing and interaction behaviour are observable signals. They are never interpreted in isolation nor exposed as standalone evaluations.
