# Observation Model

## Purpose

IMAGO builds a Professional Representation from observable evidence.

Observations are never judgements.

Multiple observations may contribute to the same Representation.

---

## OBS-001 — Professional presentation

**Observation:** Free presentation of the professional journey.
**Purpose:** Establish the initial professional image.
**Visible:** No

---

## OBS-002 — Narrative structure

**Observation:** Order and organisation of the professional story.
**Purpose:** Assess awareness of one's professional journey.
**Visible:** No

---

## OBS-003 — Initial response time

**Observation:** Time between the question and the beginning of the response.
**Purpose:** Contextual behavioural signal. Never interpreted in isolation.
**Visible:** No

---

## OBS-004 — Response duration

**Observation:** Total response duration.
**Purpose:** Contextual behavioural signal. Never interpreted in isolation.
**Visible:** No

---

## OBS-005 — Adaptive probing

**Observation:** Additional questions required to reach sufficient observability.
**Purpose:** Measure remaining uncertainty.
**Visible:** No

---

## OBS-006 — Supporting material

**Observation:** CV and additional professional documents.
**Purpose:** Increase Representation completeness.
**Visible:** Partially

---

## Evolution

New observations may be introduced without changing existing identifiers.
