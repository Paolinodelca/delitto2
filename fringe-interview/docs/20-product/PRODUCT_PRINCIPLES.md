# IMAGO Product Principles

## P-01 — Person ownership
A personal Representation belongs to the person it describes.

## P-02 — Delegated access
Tutor, coach or outplacement operator receives explicit, limited and revocable access. Ownership never transfers.

## P-03 — Evidence before interpretation
Material conclusions remain traceable to evidence, context, provenance and uncertainty.

## P-04 — Non-observed is not absent
Lack of evidence never becomes an automatic negative judgement.

## P-05 — Representation is not reality
A Representation is the best available model within a declared context and time.

## P-06 — No person score
IMAGO may estimate dimensions or contextual fit, but does not reduce a person to one global value.

## P-07 — Persistent evolution
Relevant updates create reconstructible dated states. History is not silently overwritten.

## P-08 — Minimal interaction
The primary path normally presents no more than two or three choices per step.

## P-09 — Progressive explanation
Explanations and detail appear only when useful or explicitly requested.

## P-10 — Product before unused hardening
Architectural downstream work is not automatically the next priority. Current user value and concrete risk drive sequencing.

## P-11 — Preserve future degrees of freedom
Solve the current problem without premature specialization or unnecessary closure of future options.

## P-12 — Privacy by model
Ownership, consent, access, revocation, retention and anonymization are domain rules, not UI decoration.

## P-13 — Observation before evaluation
Observations are signals. They are not judgements and are never interpreted in isolation when context matters.

## P-14 — Perspective does not rewrite knowledge
Recruiter, Tutor, interviewer, role or organization perspectives interpret available knowledge but do not mutate the underlying evidence.
