# IMAGO Product Principles

## P-01 — Person ownership
A personal Representation belongs to the person it describes.

## P-02 — Delegated access
Tutor, coach or outplacement operator receives explicit, limited and revocable access. They do not own the Representation.

## P-03 — Evidence before conclusions
Every material conclusion must remain explainable through source, evidence, context and uncertainty.

## P-04 — Non-observed is not absent
Lack of evidence must never become an automatic negative judgment.

## P-05 — Persistent evolution
A Representation may evolve over time. Updates must not silently erase relevant history.

## P-06 — Product before unused hardening
Architectural downstream work is not automatically the next priority.

## P-07 — Minimal interaction
Each step should normally require no more than two or three choices.

## P-08 — Progressive explanation
Help appears only when needed through concise inline or expandable guidance.

## P-09 — Preserve future degrees of freedom
Solve the current problem without irreversible specialization or premature generalization.

## P-10 — Privacy by model
Ownership, consent, access, revocation, retention and anonymization are domain rules, not UI decoration.
