# IMAGO Professional Domain Model

## Core entities

### Person
Owner of the personal Representation.

### Professional Identity
Persistent professional Representation of the person.

### Representation Snapshot
Dated, reconstructible state of the Professional Identity.

### Source Material
CV, LinkedIn, documents, notes, job descriptions, interview answers and other inputs.

### Tutor
Authorized collaborator who may read, contribute, comment or operate within granted permissions.

### Permission
Explicit, scoped and revocable authorization granted by the person.

### Session
Bounded interaction such as interview, review, analysis or update.

### Output
Report, CV proposal, cover letter, action plan or other application view.

## Ownership rule
The person owns the Professional Identity. The tutor owns only their own annotations and actions.

## Beta simplification
The Private Beta may implement only:
- one person;
- one Professional Identity;
- optional tutor access;
- simple permission states;
- dated snapshots;
- limited outputs.
