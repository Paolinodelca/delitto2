Job Description — Product Operations Manager

We are looking for a Product Operations Manager to support the scalability, consistency and execution quality of our product organization.

This role sits at the intersection of Product, Operations, Customer Success and Data. The ideal candidate will help improve decision-making, prioritization, process clarity and cross-functional execution across the product lifecycle.

The Product Operations Manager will work closely with Product Managers, Business Analysts, Operations stakeholders and leadership to make product work more measurable, more aligned and easier to execute at scale.

Key responsibilities
Build and improve product operating processes across planning, prioritization and execution.
Support the product team in defining workflows, decision frameworks and consistent ways of working.
Partner with Product Managers to improve visibility on roadmap progress, dependencies and delivery risks.
Translate recurring operational issues and stakeholder friction into structured improvement initiatives.
Coordinate cross-functional initiatives involving Product, Operations, Support, Customer Success and Data teams.
Help define and monitor core product and operational KPIs.
Drive cadence around reporting, action tracking and follow-up on critical product initiatives.
Improve communication quality between product and non-product stakeholders.
Contribute to product reviews, leadership updates and decision support materials.
Requirements
5+ years of experience in Product Operations, Business Operations, Program Management, Business Analysis or similar cross-functional roles.
Strong ability to work across teams and align stakeholders with different priorities.
Solid analytical mindset and comfort working with KPIs, dashboards and structured reporting.
Experience improving processes, workflows or governance in complex environments.
Ability to move between strategic discussions and operational follow-through.
Clear communication skills, including the ability to synthesize complexity into actionable decisions.
Experience in SaaS, digital products or tech-enabled environments is preferred.
Familiarity with SQL, BI tools or structured analytical tooling is a plus.
What good looks like in this role

A strong candidate is not just good at analysis. They are able to create order across ambiguity, make collaboration smoother, improve execution discipline and help product teams operate with more consistency and impact.
