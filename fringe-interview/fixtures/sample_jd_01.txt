Job Title: Product Operations Manager

We are looking for a Product Operations Manager to support alignment between product, operations, and commercial teams in a fast-paced B2B SaaS environment.

The role will help improve operational visibility, drive KPI reporting, and support execution of cross-functional initiatives. You will work closely with product managers and business stakeholders to identify friction points, improve processes, and help teams make better data-informed decisions.

Key responsibilities:
- Own recurring operational and product performance reporting
- Build and improve dashboards for internal stakeholders
- Partner with Product, Sales, and Customer Success to improve execution
- Translate business needs into reporting and workflow improvements
- Support prioritization by identifying operational inefficiencies and recurring issues
- Present insights and updates to senior stakeholders
- Help drive accountability across teams for key initiatives

Requirements:
- 4+ years of experience in operations, business analysis, product operations, or a similar role
- Strong SQL skills
- Experience with BI tools such as Power BI or Tableau
- Strong stakeholder management and cross-functional collaboration
- Ability to work in fast-paced environments with changing priorities
- Strong communication and structured problem-solving skills

Preferred:
- Experience in SaaS or B2B software environments
- Experience working closely with Product teams
- Experience owning processes or initiatives end-to-end
- Familiarity with customer lifecycle or success operations

Nice to have:
- Experience mentoring junior team members
- Experience with workflow design or operational playbooks

This role is ideal for someone who combines analytical discipline with operational judgment and can create clarity across multiple teams.
