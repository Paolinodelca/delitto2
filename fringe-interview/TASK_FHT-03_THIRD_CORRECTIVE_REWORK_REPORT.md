## Reason for the third corrective rework

The third live Marco run verified the main structured invariants introduced by the previous corrective reworks:

- CandidateProfile preserves `leadershipExposure = "unclear"` and `peopleLeadership = "unclear"`;
- JobFit structured `gaps`, `weakSignals`, and `missingSkills` no longer carry the unauthorized Six Sigma/certification or unclear-leadership deficiencies;
- leadership remains available for clarification / acquisition surfaces;
- Six Sigma no longer survives as a factual structured target/person deficiency.

However, the live JobFit still contained a pre-enforcement `fitSummary.shortRationale` that negatively cited Six Sigma despite the absence of target requirement authority. Because this rationale is consumed downstream by FinalCandidateReport, ProReportModel, renderer, and interview-plan code, the stale prose remained a blocking semantic leak even though the structured JobFit had been sanitized.

## Root cause

`fitSummary.shortRationale` is generated by the LLM together with the structured JobFit, before `enforceJobFitSemanticIntegrity()` runs. The existing deterministic enforcement corrected `gaps`, `missingSkills`, `weakSignals`, `reportHighlights.risks`, and unclear target-requirement handling, but did not invalidate the already-generated prose.

Therefore the pipeline could become inconsistent:

`sanitized structured JobFit` + `unsanitized pre-enforcement rationale`.

## Boundary and enforcement chosen

The fix remains at the existing narrow JobFit semantic boundary:

`src/parser/enforceFht03SemanticIntegrity.js`

No downstream consumer was modified.

After the existing structured enforcement and clarification preservation, `fitSummary.shortRationale` is deterministically invalidated by setting it to an empty string.

This is intentionally fail-closed. The repository does not expose a canonical deterministic natural-language authority capable of proving each clause of an LLM-generated rationale safe after structured sanitation. Attempting to parse and rewrite arbitrary prose would introduce the generalized natural-language interpretation layer explicitly excluded by the task. A new generative pass or prompt was also excluded.

The safe fallback therefore removes the pre-enforcement rationale at the semantic boundary rather than allowing stale unsupported claims to propagate downstream.

No user-facing replacement copy was hardcoded in code.

## Why this satisfies the required semantic invariant

After enforcement:

- unauthorized structured target/person deficiencies are removed;
- target requirements with candidate state `unclear` remain clarification/acquisition concerns rather than factual missing capability;
- HELP surfaces remain untouched;
- `shortRationale` can no longer preserve a negative claim that the deterministic structured boundary rejected;
- downstream consumers automatically receive a JobFit whose pre-generated summary prose cannot contradict the enforced factual state.
