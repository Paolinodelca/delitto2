# IMAGO EMPLOYER — STRATEGIC EXPLORATION

Status: STRATEGIC EXPLORATION — NOT PRODUCT AUTHORITY — NOT IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP
Date: 2026-09-16

## Purpose

Preserve the strategic direction emerged during Beta value work without changing current Product Authority or diverting the Candidate Beta roadmap.

The hypothesis is that the same IMAGO Core and Living Professional Identity model may support a second product side for employers, recruiters and hiring managers.

The near-term objective remains validation of Candidate-side value. Employer concepts should influence architecture only by avoiding unnecessary future lock-in; they do not authorize implementation.

---

## North Star

A professional person should not be independently reconstructed as dozens of partial candidate profiles owned by different employers.

The long-term hypothesis is:

**one person → one person-owned Living Professional Identity → multiple authorised, purpose/context-specific Professional Representations.**

The Living Professional Identity remains owned and governed by the person.

Companies may access or discover only authorised representations under explicit purposes and access/discovery boundaries.

The company does not own or silently mutate the person's Professional Identity.

A company may instead maintain its own separate Company ↔ Person relationship history and hiring-process memory.

## Three horizons

### Horizon 1 — Candidate product

Continue validating the current Candidate-side product:

- persistent Living Professional Identity;
- richer Professional Representation;
- explainability and provenance;
- Career Direction exploration;
- targeted acquisition;
- Interview;
- future Evidence Challenges where separately authorised.

This remains the active product-development priority.

### Horizon 2 — IMAGO Employer

Plausible B2B product hypothesis:

**Evidence-Grounded Hiring Intelligence**

A company brings an Opportunity and candidates. IMAGO helps the company understand the Opportunity, understand available professional material, identify uncertainty, acquire only useful additional evidence, compare explainably and support a human hiring decision.

Possible workflow:

Opportunity/JD
→ Opportunity Understanding
→ concise iterative clarification with employer
→ Opportunity Representation
→ authorised search perimeter
→ Candidate/Professional Representations
→ relevant evidence + uncertainty
→ selective evidence acquisition
→ explainable comparison
→ HR progressive selection
→ human final decision
→ Company Hiring Memory.

The intended search perimeter may include:

- received applications;
- people already known to the company;
- company-local talent pool;
- in the longer term, Professional Identities that have authorised IMAGO-network discovery.

### Horizon 3 — Professional Identity Network

Long-term network hypothesis:

People maintain their own Living Professional Identity because it creates direct Candidate-side value.

People may optionally authorise discovery for selected professional contexts/opportunities.

Employers describe an Opportunity and IMAGO may discover authorised Professional Identities whose supported professional material is relevant.

Discovery does not imply full access. The person remains able to decide whether to engage and what further information to share.

Potential flywheel:

person obtains value
→ builds/updates Living PI
→ optionally authorises discovery
→ employer searches/pays
→ Opportunity identifies relevant evidence and uncertainty
→ employer requests bounded deepening
→ person updates/adds evidence
→ Living PI becomes richer
→ Candidate and Employer value increase.

Do not assume marketplace/network launch before single-sided value is validated.

## Opportunity Understanding

Employer-side analysis should not start by ranking people against an unexamined Job Description.

Preferred hypothesis:

JD / employer need
→ IMAGO returns a concise interpretation
→ identifies only decision-relevant ambiguity
→ asks a small number of clarifying questions
→ iterates if necessary
→ produces a compact agreed Opportunity Representation.

Each loop must remain concise. Internal sophistication must not become a multi-page burden for HR.

The Opportunity Representation should distinguish at least:

- professionally relevant requirements/aspects;
- genuinely central vs useful/differentiating aspects;
- operational constraints;
- unresolved Opportunity ambiguity.

Do not collapse these into one generic score.

## Candidate understanding and progressive selection

Employer UI should support progressive human selection, for example:

153 candidates
→ HR selects a smaller group for deeper review
→ selective acquisition
→ HR selects finalists
→ optional Interview / Evidence Challenge
→ HR chooses whom to progress/hire.

IMAGO supplies evidence, relations, uncertainty, provenance and explanations.

The human employer makes the selection decisions.

The interface should be concise first and progressively explainable:

**meaning / synthesis → why this person is worth deeper review → what remains to verify → optional expansion → evidence/provenance.**

This repeats a UX grammar already human-validated in Candidate Career Direction and is a candidate for broader Product validation, not yet a canonical Product Decision.

Employer candidate view should allow access, when authorised, to recent CVs, positions and deeper Professional Representation material.

## Evidence Basis / credibility problem

A rich self-description is not equivalent to demonstrated professional capability.

IMAGO must distinguish at least conceptually:

1. what the person declares;
2. how the declaration has been explored/corroborated;
3. what has actually been observed in an appropriate controlled professional interaction or simulation;
4. what remains uncertain.

Do not create an AI lie detector, sincerity score or emotion-based credibility judgment.

A future user-facing **Evidence Basis** may communicate the nature and robustness of support without pretending to know the person's hidden traits.

Possible evidence-basis categories to explore, not yet canonical:

- declared / source-reported;
- professionally elaborated through bounded questioning;
- supported by multiple independent sources/evidence;
- observed in an authorised Interview / Evidence Challenge;
- insufficient / conflicting / unresolved.

These are not automatically a single ordinal scale.

A stronger or more numerous signal must remain distinct from the quality/type of evidence supporting it.

## Comparative employer view

Employers need an efficient comparative view.

Candidate hypothesis:

- rows = Opportunity aspects confirmed with employer;
- columns = selected candidates;
- visual signal = amount/consistency of relevant supported material;
- separate visual semantics = Evidence Basis / observability / unresolved uncertainty;
- click-through = why → professional material → evidence → provenance → limitations.

A short bar must never silently mean low capability.

It may mean insufficient or weakly supported information.

**Not observed ≠ absent.**

Avoid opaque universal fit percentages as the primary representation.

Operational sorting/filtering may be explored only through explicit, inspectable Opportunity-relative criteria under human control.

## Adaptive Evidence Acquisition

Strategic hypothesis:

IMAGO should not default to giving every candidate the same large battery of tests.

It should first ask:

**What additional professional information is genuinely worth acquiring before the next human decision?**

Possible acquisition ladder:

- targeted question;
- asynchronous bounded deepening;
- IMAGO Interview;
- Standardized Evidence Challenge;
- Adaptive Evidence Challenge;
- Hybrid Evidence Challenge.

### Standardized Evidence Challenge

Same core scenario for finalists when comparability is important.

### Adaptive Evidence Challenge

Different bounded scenario/probes chosen because a specific candidate has a specific decision-relevant uncertainty.

Primary purpose is uncertainty reduction, not universal scoring.

### Hybrid Evidence Challenge

Common core scenario
+
adaptive follow-ups based on the candidate's decisions, available Professional Identity material and unresolved conditions.

This may combine comparability with individual depth.

Evidence Challenges should use realistic professional situations, incomplete information, changing conditions, trade-offs and observable decision traces where appropriate to the role/seniority.

They must not infer personality, emotion, sincerity, hidden traits or universal capability without separate authorised semantic authority and adequate evidence.

## Candidate response to employer deepening

Employer may request deeper information.

Employer UI should show a state such as:

**request sent — awaiting person response**

with a realistic multi-day timeout rather than assuming immediate response.

Candidate receives enough information to make an informed decision, potentially including:

- company identity or bounded company description where confidentiality applies;
- sector;
- approximate size/context;
- geographic area;
- Opportunity / JD;
- why IMAGO believes the Opportunity may merit attention;
- time available to respond/update.

Candidate may:

- express interest;
- request more information;
- decline;
- optionally give a reason for declining;
- update the Living PI;
- answer targeted acquisition requests.

Decline reasons/preferences belong to preference/context, not Professional Knowledge.

Future messaging/chat channels may accelerate response but must not be assumed as the only workflow.

## Free / Premium / Employer economic hypothesis

Long-term hypothesis:

### Free

Free should potentially represent membership/maintenance of the Professional Identity layer, not merely a crippled Premium tier.

Possible Free rights/services:

- persistent Living PI;
- essential updates;
- privacy/discovery controls;
- receive authorised Opportunity requests;
- respond to employer-requested bounded deepening;
- maintain ownership of professional identity.

The Living PI should not disappear because the person stops paying.

### Candidate Premium

Possible paid value:

- richer Career Direction exploration;
- deeper Professional Representation/insight;
- application/CV tailoring;
- Interview Practice;
- advanced transition/coaching workflows;
- other high-value personal services.

### Employer

Employer pays for services around the Opportunity and decision workflow, potentially including:

- Opportunity Understanding;
- candidate understanding/discovery;
- explainable relevance;
- selective acquisition;
- Interview / Evidence Challenges;
- comparative decision support;
- hiring workflow;
- Company Hiring Memory.

Possible commercial unit to investigate:

**Hiring Intelligence Campaign per Opportunity**, with additional usage/cost for expensive acquisition steps.

Do not assume final pricing/model without validation.

## Company ↔ Person relationship

Company-specific knowledge about its relationship with a person must remain separate from the person's Living PI.

Examples may include, subject to governance and lawful retention:

- previously considered;
- previously interviewed;
- stage reached;
- recontact permission/status;
- company-local notes;
- prior Opportunity relation.

A company's judgment must not silently become Person Knowledge or become visible to unrelated companies.

---

## Company Hiring Memory

Potential future B2B asset:

Preserve the company's hiring-process history separately from Person Knowledge.

Possible data:

- Opportunity aspects initially declared important;
- clarification/changes during Opportunity Understanding;
- criteria used by HR during progressive selection;
- reasons explicitly recorded for shortlist/final decisions;
- later, only with adequate methodology, carefully bounded links to post-hire outcomes.

Potential future value:

help a company understand how it actually makes hiring decisions and where declared criteria differ from criteria repeatedly used in practice.

Do not convert recruiter preference into a permanent trait of a candidate.

Do not claim predictive validity from selection history alone.

---

## Market / ecosystem intelligence

A larger IMAGO ecosystem may create aggregated information about professional roles, requirements, changing opportunity patterns and hiring questions.

This may connect in the future with the previously parked Market Intelligence / Observatory concept.

However:

- personal data must not become a hidden resale product;
- aggregation/anonymisation, purpose, lawful basis, consent where required, minimisation and retention require explicit design;
- company-specific confidential criteria must remain appropriately scoped;
- market intelligence must remain distinct from Person Knowledge.

## Market observations from 2026 strategic scan

The following categories already exist and are competitive:

- ATS / applicant tracking;
- AI candidate matching and screening;
- talent rediscovery / talent pools;
- talent intelligence;
- structured AI interviews;
- situational judgement tests;
- job simulations / virtual job tryouts;
- AI-assisted assessment generation from job descriptions;
- automated assessment scoring.

Examples reviewed during exploration included LinkedIn Talent Solutions, Workday HiredScore, Eightfold AI, Personio, Vervoe, TestGorilla, HireVue, Harver, SHL and Sova.

Therefore the following are NOT sufficient differentiation on their own:

- AI CV screening;
- candidate ranking;
- talent-pool storage;
- explainable match alone;
- AI Interview alone;
- realistic job simulation alone.

Potential IMAGO differentiation to investigate:

**Living person-owned Professional Identity + evidence/provenance + Person/Target separation + explicit uncertainty + selective acquisition of only decision-relevant missing evidence + explainable contextual Representation.**

This is a strategic hypothesis, not a validated market claim.

## Regulatory / trust direction

Employment/recruiting AI is a high-impact/high-governance domain, particularly in the EU.

Employer-side IMAGO should be designed as human decision support, not an opaque autonomous hiring judge.

Avoid as design defaults:

- automatic rejection based on opaque AI output;
- universal person score;
- emotion recognition;
- sincerity/lie-detection claims;
- protected/sensitive trait inference;
- uninspectable ranking;
- treating missing evidence as negative capability evidence.

A future Employer product requires dedicated legal, privacy, fairness, validation, auditability and human-oversight review before productisation.

## Strategic validation questions

Before moving Employer into Product Authority, validate at least:

1. Do occasional/mid-market hirers experience enough pain in high-value hiring to pay for deeper explainable candidate understanding?
2. Is the strongest initial buyer an occasional-hiring SME, mid-market HR team, recruiter/search firm, outplacement ecosystem or another segment?
3. Does selective evidence acquisition outperform the perceived value/cost of standard assessment batteries?
4. Will candidates maintain a Living PI if Free preserves identity and paid services sit above it?
5. Will people opt into professional discovery under understandable controls?
6. Can Employer receive enough value before a large IMAGO network exists?
7. Can Evidence Basis be made useful without becoming a misleading credibility/person score?
8. Can Hybrid Evidence Challenges provide useful comparability while preserving adaptive depth?
9. What governance is required for Company Hiring Memory and aggregated market intelligence?
10. Does this become an adjacent B2B product, a second market side, or eventually a two-sided professional network?
